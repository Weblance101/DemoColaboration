OUR STORY

Crafting Excellence in Islamic Architecture

TAJ MINAR & TAJ SALES is dedicated to providing complete mosque and Islamic architectural solutions. We bring together quality craftsmanship, thoughtful design, and reliable products to serve mosques, madrasas, and Islamic institutions.

From beautifully designed minars and domes to mimbars, wuzu facilities, carpets, and Islamic essentials, we aim to make every project meaningful and long-lasting.

Our focus is on quality, customer satisfaction, and building lasting relationships through dependable service.
